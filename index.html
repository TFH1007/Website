About Me

Hi, I'm Tahsin.

I'm an ML engineer and mechatronics graduate from Malaysia, now based in New York. I did my undergrad and first master's at the International Islamic University Malaysia, where I studied Mechatronics Engineering and got my hands dirty with PLC systems, sensor circuits, robotics, and industrial automation. Then I came to the University at Buffalo for a second master's in Computer Science, where I joined the WIRES Lab and started building machine learning systems for wireless sensing.

My research sits at the intersection of signal processing and deep learning. I design models from first principles, which means I care about the math behind why things work, not just whether they produce good numbers on a benchmark. My main project, FedWiLoc, went from a whiteboard idea to a deployed system running on a Raspberry Pi in under a year, and the work has been published at AAAI 2026 with a submission to ACM TIOT 2026.

Outside of research, I taught debate and public speaking in Malaysia, served as President of the MUN Club at IIUM (we organized the university's first conference), and I'm currently VP of the Bangladeshi Graduate Student Association at UB. I design event posters, take photographs, and paint when I get the time.

I'm looking for ML Engineer, Data Scientist, or Data Engineer roles in the NYC metro area. If you're hiring and made it this far, I'd love to talk.
